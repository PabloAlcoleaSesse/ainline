# Code of Conduct

We want ainline to be welcoming to contributors of different backgrounds,
experience levels, and perspectives.

Be respectful, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Help
newcomers ask questions. Give credit for others' work. Respect privacy and avoid
sharing anyone's personal information without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and
repeated personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions,
or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or ban
participants when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior
and its impact. Maintainers are expected to follow these standards too.

For a concern, contact the maintainer through the contact options on
[PabloAlcoleaSesse's GitHub profile](https://github.com/PabloAlcoleaSesse). Do not post
private details or accusations in a public issue; ask for a private contact channel
first if one is not available. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
